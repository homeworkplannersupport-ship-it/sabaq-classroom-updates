# Sabaq Workspace Build 7

Build 7 replaces account invitation codes with conventional Firebase email/password accounts. New users verify their email, choose a student, teacher, or guardian profile, and enter the matching role-specific workspace. Administrator access remains protected.

Students add their age during profile setup. Age is synchronized through Firebase and shown only to authenticated members of the same classroom.

Classroom invitations are now separate from account creation. Teachers and administrators can invite an existing student or teacher by account email. The recipient accepts or declines inside Sabaq Workspace, and acceptance updates the live Firebase roster across devices.
