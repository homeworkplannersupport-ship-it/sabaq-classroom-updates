# Sabaq Workspace Build 9

New accounts stay signed in on a dedicated verification waiting page. The app checks Firebase automatically and advances as soon as the email address is verified, with manual check and resend controls available if needed.

Every password form can show or hide the text currently being entered. Settings now provides secure Firebase password-change and reset-email actions; existing saved passwords remain unreadable by design.

The new Support and Feedback section opens a reviewable email addressed to sabaqworkspace.support@gmail.com and includes the app build, account role, and Android version to make troubleshooting easier.

Verification guidance now calls out Spam and Promotions folders. Inbox placement is ultimately decided by the recipient's email provider and cannot be guaranteed by the Android app.

New classrooms now appear immediately without reopening the app. Classroom managers can invite co-teachers, and assignment or stream-post publishing closes the composer immediately while the secure upload continues in the background.

Classrooms, assignments, and stream announcements now support permanent deletion. A five-second warning clearly states that the action is irreversible, and the server removes dependent submissions, grades, feedback, comments, revisions, progress, roster links, invitations, and uploaded files as appropriate.
